Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026.

The only user of Hermes is the owner.

Data accessed

Hermes reads the owner's own Google Health weight and body-fat measurements, activity and fitness data, and basic profile, through these read-only scopes:

  • googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly
  • googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly
  • googlehealth.profile.readonly

Hermes also reads the owner's own WHOOP data.

Use

Data is used only to generate the owner's personal health summaries.

Storage

Data is kept privately on the owner's own computer. It is never sold or shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train models.

Hermes's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Data is deleted on request.
