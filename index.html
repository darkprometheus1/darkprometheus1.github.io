DarkPrometheus Analytics

A private, read-only tool the owner of the DarkPrometheus YouTube channel uses to view their own channel analytics.

Privacy Policy

This app requests read-only access to YouTube channel and analytics data for the channel owner's own account. The data is used only to make performance reports for the owner. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
